Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Pi Challenge ("the app") is a game where you try to memorize digits of pi. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

  • On your device only: your name, best score, attempt count, records and settings are saved locally on your device. The app has no accounts and does not send this information to us or any server.
  • Extra digits: the first 100,000 digits of pi are built into the app. If you play beyond that, the app requests further digits from the public service api.pi.delivery, which receives your IP address like any web request.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob (mobile) and Google AdSense (web). Google and its partners may collect and use device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address and approximate location to serve and measure ads. On iOS, the app asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you decline, ads are not personalized. You can reset or limit the advertising ID in your device settings. See Google's advertising policy for details.

What we don't do

We don't require an account, and we don't collect your email, contacts, photos or precise location. We don't sell personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them.

Data deletion

Because your data stays on your device, you can delete it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
